Chapter One

It Started Somehow...

Pata nahi kab tum meri life ka ek normal sa hissa se… meri favourite ban gayi. Shuruaat toh bilkul simple thi.....kuch random conversations, thodi si nok-jhok, kuch hasi-mazaak aur woh chhoti-chhoti baatein jo us waqt normal lagti thi. Lekin dheere-dheere tumhari messages ka wait hone laga… tumhari smile important lagne lagi… aur tumhara mood mere mood ko affect karne laga. Mujhe khud nahi pata chala ki kab mera tumse baat karna meri favourite part of the day ban gaya. Shayad pyaar kisi ek moment mein nahi hua tha… woh toh har chhoti conversation, har smile aur har little moment ke saath thoda-thoda hota gaya. Aur phir ek din realize hua— main tumhe sirf pasand nahi karta… main tumhe apni story ka sabse khoobsurat chapter maan chuka hoon.... ❤️
